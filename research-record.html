Registry protocol

How another researcher’s work is added

The graph is designed to grow without blurring authorship, version history, or evidential scope.

Identify the exact record

Provide the title, all authors, version, publication date, venue, DOI or stable public URL, and the exact Torus-Node version being referenced.

Declare the relationship

Choose a directional relation such as cites, extends, verifies, challenges, or supplies a counterexample. A relation is descriptive, not an endorsement.

Preserve attribution

Independent work remains attributed to its own authors and host. Corrections create an auditable update rather than silently rewriting the research record.
