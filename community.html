Scientific norms

How constructive scrutiny works here

  • Quote the precise statement, version, and assumptions under discussion.
  • Separate algebraic consistency, physical modeling, and empirical validation.
  • Provide enough detail for another researcher to reproduce the claim.
  • Treat counterexamples and corrected errors as valuable research outputs.
  • Disclose computational tools, approximations, and external dependencies.
  • Credit prior work and distinguish adaptation from original contribution.

Participation is governed by the repository’s Code of Conduct and contribution protocol.

Independent results

External contributions remain attributable

Researchers may submit a citation and reproducibility record for work hosted elsewhere. Inclusion indicates relevance and traceability, not endorsement of every conclusion.
