# Contributing to the Torus-Node Research Initiative

We welcome rigorous support, criticism, corrections, counterexamples, independent calculations, and formal code. Contributions are evaluated on technical merit and reproducibility, not on agreement with the model.

## Where to contribute

- Use **Discussions** for open-ended questions, interpretation, literature links, and early research proposals.
- Use **Issues** for actionable verification tasks, possible errors, counterexamples, defined open problems, independent results, and website improvements.
- Use **Pull Requests** for concrete changes that can be reviewed as a diff.

The Lean formalisation remains in the separate [`torus-node-lean4`](https://github.com/s6ygleee/torus-node-lean4) repository. Propose Lean changes there unless the change concerns only this portal’s documentation.

## Required technical record

Every scientific contribution should include:

1. the exact statement, version, and location addressed;
2. all mathematical, physical, and computational assumptions;
3. a derivation, proof, algorithm, or counterexample;
4. code, inputs, outputs, and environment details when computation is used;
5. a scope statement explaining what follows and what does not;
6. citations and attribution for prior work.

## Possible errors and counterexamples

Quote the smallest statement that may fail. Show that your example satisfies its hypotheses. Separate formal errors, semantic mismatches, numerical problems, citation issues, and physical objections. Responsible negative results are welcome.

## Pull requests

- Keep the change focused.
- Preserve the separation between established, conditional, assumed, conjectural, and open claims.
- Do not add institutional affiliations or endorsements without a verifiable primary source.
- Check internal links and mobile layout.
- Explain how you validated the change.

## Attribution and independent results

State author names, affiliations only when authorized, persistent identifiers when available, and the license or reuse terms of submitted material. Listing an external result indicates relevance and traceability, not endorsement.

## Conduct

Follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Critique claims and methods rather than people. Do not use credentials, private data, or unpublished material without authorization.
