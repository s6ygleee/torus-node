# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Torus-Node Research Initiative is committed to a professional research environment where participants can test ideas, report errors, and disagree on technical grounds without harassment or retaliation.

## Expected conduct

- Address claims with evidence, definitions, and reproducible reasoning.
- Represent assumptions, uncertainty, and authorship accurately.
- Credit prior work and correct the record when an attribution is incomplete.
- Welcome good-faith criticism, counterexamples, and negative results.
- Keep discussion relevant and avoid repetitive advocacy after a point has been answered.
- Protect confidential information, credentials, and personal data.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation, plagiarism, fabricated evidence, doxxing, spam, and publication of private material are not acceptable.

## Scientific disagreement

Moderation does not decide whether a theory is true. Maintainers may close or redirect threads that lack a falsifiable claim, ignore stated assumptions, duplicate existing discussion, or cannot be made reproducible. Such moderation is organizational, not scientific endorsement.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options available on the GitHub profile. Do not use a public Issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community standards and adapted for evidence-based scientific discussion.
