Research program

A finite construction with explicit boundaries

The initiative separates exact finite constructions from conditional physical interpretations, empirical comparisons, and unresolved continuum questions.

01 / Geometry

Discrete torus-node geometry

Nodes carry area, modular shape, rotor phase, internal state, and winding data. A proposed bounded completion organizes admissible relations and moves.

02 / Algebra

D4 moves and S4 histories

The finite model relates spatial moves, level-three cusp data, determinant-two correspondences, and an ordered-history algebra.

03 / Physics

Conditional low-energy structure

Gauge, matter, and effective gravitational forms are investigated under stated completion, reconstruction, and derivative-expansion assumptions.
